# Swimming Resources

## Knowledge

- [Article: "Seamless Breathing" by Total Immersion](https://www.totalimmersion.net/blog/seamless-breathing/)
  The one-goggle breath: head stays in line with the spine, and you roll the body to the air instead of lifting. Use for: the inhale, and any later cue about the head.
- [Guide: "Freestyle Breathing" by U.S. Masters Swimming](https://www.usms.org/fitness-and-training/guides/freestyle/breathing)
  Lifting the head drops the hips. Use for: why a small roll is the whole skill, before any stroke work.
- Book: _Total Immersion_ by Terry Laughlin.
  Exhale continuously with the face in the water, and keep the head down so the hips stay up. Use for: the mission's definition of a calm length.

## Wisdom (Communities)
